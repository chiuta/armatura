Third-party texts

docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md reproduces, verbatim, licence and notice files that
the authors of third-party Rust crates ship with their packages. Each text keeps
the licence of its own author (named in the heading above it in that file);
none of it is covered by the CC-BY-4.0 that applies to the rest of the
documentation, and none of it is written by the OSATURA author.
