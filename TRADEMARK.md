The licences in LICENSE cover the code, not the name "OSATURA" or any logo. (Check trademark availability before launch; "osatura" is also an ordinary Romanian/Italian word.)

Allowed without asking: saying that your software is "compatible with OSATURA" or "made with OSATURA"; redistributing an unmodified build under the original name; linking to the project.

Not allowed without permission: distributing a modified build under the name OSATURA or in a way that suggests it is official; using the name or logo as your own product or company name.
